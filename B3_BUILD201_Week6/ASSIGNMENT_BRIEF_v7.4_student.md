# BUILD 201 — Week 6 Reflection (Submit via Canvas)

**Due:** 11:59 p.m. Friday  
**Length:** 300–400 words  
**Format:** Continuous prose (no bullet lists in the submission box)

## Task

Reflect on **one idea from this week's studio activity** (peer demo day). Explain why it stayed with you, how it changed your thinking, and one limitation or trade-off you now see. Connect briefly to a course theme from Weeks 4–6 (trust, workflow design, or evaluation).

## Tips

- Name a **specific moment** from the demo, not only a general topic.
- Go beyond summary: say **how** your thinking changed.
- One honest **limitation or trade-off** strengthens the reflection.

## Submitting in Canvas

Paste your reflection into the assignment text box. Proofread for clarity; grammar is not heavily weighted (see rubric).
