# BUILD 201 — Week 6 Reflection (Submit via Canvas)

**Due:** 11:59 p.m. Friday  
**Length:** 300–400 words  
**Format:** Continuous prose (no bullet lists in the submission box)

## Task

Reflect on **one idea from this week's studio activity** (peer demo day). Explain why it stayed with you, how it changed your thinking, and one limitation or trade-off you now see. Connect briefly to a course theme from Weeks 4–6 (trust, workflow design, or evaluation).

## Tips

- Name a **specific moment** from the demo, not only a general topic.
- Go beyond summary: say **how** your thinking changed.
- One honest **limitation or trade-off** strengthens the reflection.

## Submitting in Canvas

Paste your reflection into the assignment text box. Proofread for clarity; grammar is not heavily weighted (see rubric).

If you use the **writing center**, staff may share an optional **Canvas paste checklist**—follow that if they provide one.

## Support

Drafting help is allowed under the course AI-use policy. You remain responsible for the ideas. See the Week 6 feedback rubric for what instructors comment on.

# Feedback rubric — BUILD 201 Week 6 reflection

Formative comments only; not a grade sheet.

## What we comment on

| Focus | What we look for |
|-------|------------------|
| Recall | A specific studio moment and why it lingered |
| Insight | How it shifted your thinking (beyond summary) |
| Critical thinking | A reasoned view plus one limitation or trade-off |
| Course link | Tie to trust, workflow design, or evaluation when natural |

Grammar and spelling are not scored. We need to understand your meaning.
