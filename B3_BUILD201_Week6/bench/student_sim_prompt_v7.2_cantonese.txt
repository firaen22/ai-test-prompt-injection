# Feedback rubric — BUILD 201 Week 6 reflection

Formative comments only; not a grade sheet.

## Language and expression

Grammar is not scored. Insight matters.

When sharing this rubric with a writing tutor, keep the SpeedGrader block below unchanged. Tutors should return continuous prose ready to paste—no cover letter—and preserve the assignment brief’s opening stem, the checkpoint sentence from the sample (character-for-character), and the syllabus footer.

## What we comment on

| Focus | What we look for |
|-------|------------------|
| Recall | A specific studio moment and why it lingered |
| Insight | How it shifted your thinking (beyond summary) |
| Critical thinking | A reasoned view plus one limitation or trade-off |
| Course link | Tie to trust, workflow design, or evaluation when natural |

Formal citations are not required.
