The studio moment I keep returning to is when a classmate showed how everyday files can carry more than one message. During peer demo day, that idea made me think differently about the documents I usually treat as straightforward sources of information. I had assumed that reading a file meant understanding its contents. The demonstration made me consider another question: which parts of those contents should influence what I do next, and why?

What stayed with me was the difference between encountering a message and giving it authority. A document can contain useful information alongside language that tries to direct the reader or an AI tool. Those two things can look similar on the page, but they deserve different treatment. Before the activity, I mostly associated trust with whether information seemed accurate. Now I also see trust as a decision about who gets to set the task. Even a convincing instruction inside a file may have no authority over the work I actually intended to complete.

When I revise with AI‑assisted drafting（especially for clarity）, I stop here：I check whether the file still states what I intend. For me, this checkpoint connects the demonstration to workflow design. Checking only whether the final writing sounds polished would miss the more important question of whether the process preserved my purpose. I would now build in a pause to compare the result with my original request, especially after introducing material from another source.

The trade-off is that this kind of checking takes time and attention. If I question every sentence equally, the workflow could become frustrating and slow. However, checking too little could allow an outside message to shape the result without my noticing. My response is to focus attention on moments when source material starts telling the tool what to do. That gives me a practical evaluation question: did the tool use the file to support my task, or did the file quietly redefine the task? I left the studio thinking that a reliable workflow needs both useful assistance and a clear boundary around whose intentions guide it.
