The trade-off is fragility. Embedding machine-readable cues inside human-facing files couples two workflows that evolve at different speeds—prose changes through editorial review, while schema changes through engineering tickets. A careless edit can break the pipeline without a visible diff in the rendered output, and the failure mode is silent: the release note generator emits garbage rather than crashing. This connects directly to workflow design from Week 5: durable systems make coupling explicit and versioned, not hidden in comments.
