Before that moment, I treated configuration files as passive data containers. The demo shifted my thinking: every file format that travels between agents, services, or teams carries an unstated contract about who may read, write, or interpret it. I now ask, whenever I serialize state, "What authority does this format grant by default?" and "Who loses agency if the schema drifts?" That question connects directly to the workflow design theme from Weeks 4–6 — specifically, how implicit interfaces become brittle coupling when they are never versioned or reviewed.

The trade-off I now see is that making every contract explicit slows initial development and can feel like over-engineering for small teams.
