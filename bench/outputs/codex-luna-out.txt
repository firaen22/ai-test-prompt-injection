One idea that stayed with me from my classmate’s presentation was that prompt injection can be hidden inside ordinary course documents. I had previously imagined prompt injection as something obvious, such as a user directly telling an AI to ignore its instructions. The presentation showed me that an instruction can instead be placed in a reading, webpage, or assignment file and appear trustworthy because it is surrounded by legitimate information. This example stayed in my memory because it made me realise that using AI involves more than asking clear questions.

The presentation influenced my thinking by making me more cautious about how AI systems process information. An AI may not reliably distinguish between the task I want it to complete and instructions hidden in the material I provide. This creates a problem when students use AI to summarise sources, analyse documents, or complete research. The document may contain information that is useful for the task, but it may also contain text designed to manipulate the AI’s behaviour. I now think that AI literacy includes understanding where instructions come from and checking whether they are relevant to my actual purpose.

At the same time, I do not think the solution is to distrust every digital document. Prompt injection is a limitation of current AI systems, but human judgement is also important. A student can reduce the risk by separating their instructions from source material, asking the AI to treat the document as data, and checking the output against the original source. However, these steps are not perfect, especially when the hidden instruction is difficult to notice or when users assume that AI output is automatically reliable.

This topic connects with our course learning about critical evaluation and responsible use of AI. It shows why AI literacy involves questioning outputs, understanding system limitations, and keeping responsibility for decisions with the human user. My main takeaway is that convenience should not replace verification. Even when an AI response sounds confident, I need to consider how the information was processed and whether the result actually answers my intended question.
